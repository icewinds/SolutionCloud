Your morning

Walk, sip, breathe

The best mornings in Tokai start the same way.

First

Take the walk

Park at Tokai Forest and set off on foot, by bike or with the dog in tow.

Then

Grab a coffee

Order at the window: a proper flat white, something from the tea list, a pastry.

Finally

Enjoy the trees

Find a spot in the shade, or take it with you. Seating is limited at peak times.
