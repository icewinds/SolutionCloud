Feature, p.02

One chair. Full attention.

Jenny keeps it small on purpose. Her studio in the Park and Shop centre in Meadowridge runs on appointments, so when you sit down, the time is yours: no rushing between clients, no waiting with wet hair.

She'll listen first, look at how your hair falls, and then suggest the cut or colour that suits your face and your routine. Then she'll show you how to style it at home.

With 86 reviews and a 4.8 rating on Google, most clients come back on schedule and bring a friend.

A cut made for how you actually wear it on a Monday morning.The idea behind the studio
