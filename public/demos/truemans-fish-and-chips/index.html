Lead story

Kirstenhof Queues Up for Fish That's "Fresh, Not Oily at All"

Over 750 Google reviews later, the little shop in the Kirstenhof Centre is still busy every day of the week.

Kirstenhof · Rated 4.3 by 757 reviewers

It's no secret along Main Road. At Trueman's, the fish is fried fresh, wrapped in a light, crispy batter and served with chips that regulars call perfectly fried. Customers come back for the friendly faces behind the counter as much as for the food.

The regulars' tip is simple: add some pickled onions to your parcel. Better still, phone your order through ahead of time, collect it hot and skip the queue that tells you you've come to the right place.

Open six days a week, with a later close on Fridays for the end-of-week fish supper.
