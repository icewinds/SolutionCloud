Aftercare

Look after it

The first 24 hours+

Keep the wrap on for as long as you were told, then wash gently with clean hands, lukewarm water and an unscented soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel.

Days 2 to 14+

Apply a thin layer of aftercare cream two or three times a day. Some flaking and itching is normal. Do not pick or scratch.

Sun, sea and swimming+

Fish Hoek beach will have to wait. Stay out of the sea, pools and direct sun until it has fully healed, then use sunscreen to keep it sharp.

Something does not look right?+

Send Steven a photo on WhatsApp. It is quicker than worrying, and you will get a straight answer.
