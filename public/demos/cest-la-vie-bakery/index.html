A morning at the bakery

Early birds get the warm ones

Everything is baked in limited batches, so the favourites can be gone before lunch. If you have your heart set on something, message us and we will put it aside.

  1. 07:30Doors open. First sourdough and croissants on the counter, coffee machine warmed up.
  2. 09:00Beach walkers and swimmers arrive. Pain au chocolat is usually still warm.
  3. 10:30The busiest stretch. Popular loaves start running low.
  4. 12:00We close, or a little earlier if the shelves are empty.
