02 / Before you call

Three quick checks.

They save time, and sometimes a call-out.

1

Check the board

Open the DB board and look for a breaker or earth leakage switch that has dropped down.

2

Unplug and reset

Unplug everything on that circuit, reset it, then plug items back one at a time.

3

Send what you see

Still tripping? WhatsApp a photo of the board and which breaker trips.

Burning smell or a hot plug? Switch that circuit off at the board and call straight away.
