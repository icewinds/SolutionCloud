Your start and finish line.

Newlands Forest is one of the best loved walks on the slopes of Table Mountain. We park right where the trails begin, so the first coffee is ready the moment you arrive and the last one is waiting when you get back.

Walkers, trail runners, dog owners and riders all welcome. Bring your keep cup.
