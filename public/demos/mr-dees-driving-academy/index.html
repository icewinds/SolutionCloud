Questions

Good to know

I'm really nervous. Is that a problem?
Not at all. Many learners start nervous. Lessons begin on quiet roads and only move on when you feel ready.
Do you pick me up?
Pick-ups around Steenberg and nearby suburbs can be arranged. Send your area on WhatsApp when you book.
How many lessons will I need?
It depends on your experience. After the first lesson, Mr Dee will give you an honest idea of how many lessons you're likely to need.
Can I book on a Sunday?
Yes, Sunday mornings from 09:00 to 12:00 are available, plus Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00.

Ready when you are.

Send a WhatsApp with your name, your area and when you'd like to start. Mr Dee will come back to you with times.
